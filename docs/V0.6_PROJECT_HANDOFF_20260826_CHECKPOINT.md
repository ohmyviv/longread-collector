## 5. Measurement-contract defects / v1.3.1 requirements

1. **Product scope must precede Recall.**
2. **Registrable domain is not publication-surface identity.** Nature journals vs Nature News, Reuters general content vs Reuters Special, and Guardian non-Long-Read sections vs Guardian Long Read are fixed counterexamples.
3. `collector_source_run_coverage` evidence must foreign-key to a successful durable run with successful full snapshot persistence/readback. Orphan coverage from a run that later fails cannot establish promotion evidence.
4. Publication-time evidence must use a hierarchy. Trusted persisted exact timestamps can resolve date-only ambiguity; conflicting high-confidence persisted timestamps must yield `publication_date_conflict` and fail closed.
5. Fixed replay anti-gaming invariant: excluding Nature journal false inclusions alone would produce a misleading `13/13=100%`; restoring the FT and Jiemian independently time-resolved true misses yields the corrected `13/15=86.7%` baseline.

The scheduled Final Recall workflow still runs v1.2. Any v1.3.1 implementation should begin offline/Shadow with fixed replay regression and must not replace scheduled v1.2 until reviewed acceptance evidence exists.

## 6. Daily Longread product-scope clarification

The user explicitly does **not** plan to include Nature-style scholarly journal papers in Daily High-Value Deep Reading.

For this product, scholarly journal assets are out of scope, including primary research papers, scholarly reviews, journal Perspective/Commentary assets and preprints. They may still be identified and stored by generic infrastructure, but Daily Longread should map them to an out-of-product-scope state and exclude them from recommendation and Recall denominators.

Scientific journalism remains in scope: Nature News/Features, Science news, Quanta, WIRED, MIT Technology Review, explanatory reporting about papers, investigations, profiles, science history and similar written journalism.

At least 7/58 current Final-reference items are academic journal assets under this product rule:

```text

```

This is a reference/product-quality issue, not a Collector discovery miss.
