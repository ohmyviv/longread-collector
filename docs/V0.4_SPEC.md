# Longread Collector v0.4 specification

Status: implementation contract  
Ground-truth batch: `LC-GT-20260729-48-v1`  
Target mode: `shadow`

## 1. Problem statement

v0.3 proves that a page can be discovered and its body can be extracted. It does not prove that the page is an editorially usable longread. In the 48-item human review, 16 rows were marked `eligible_for_editor=TRUE`, but only eight retained any editorial or source-chase value. A/B extraction verification therefore remains a technical signal, not an editorial disposition.

v0.4 adds a semantic candidate-processing layer between extraction and the daily editor.

## 2. Non-goals

- v0.4 does not select the final daily ten articles.
- v0.4 does not replace the 07:35 editor while running in shadow mode.
- The fixed 48-item ground truth is never injected into daily candidates.
- `cache_primary` remains disabled until both transport and editorial gates pass and a separate shadow A/B period is complete.

## 3. Processing stages

1. Discover URLs from directed sources and open-web queries.
2. Classify discovery results as standalone content, a source-chase lead, or non-content.
3. Extract and technically verify standalone content.
4. Classify content type and source relationship.
5. Resolve the best available original/canonical source.
6. Cluster exact, wire-service, translated, and near-duplicate items.
7. Emit one structured candidate disposition.
8. Persist all evidence and shadow metrics to Google Sheets.

- `duplicate_type`
- `content_cluster_id`
- `classification_confidence`
- `classification_version`

`hosting_source` identifies the page host. `canonical_source` identifies the best editorial source currently resolved. They must not be populated from one shared domain value when contrary evidence is present.

## 6. Backward compatibility

`eligible_for_editor` remains in the Sheet during shadow migration, but becomes a derived field:

```text
eligible_for_editor = candidate_disposition == formal_candidate
```

A/B/C/D continues to represent extraction and metadata verification only. It must never be used by itself as editorial eligibility.

## 7. Discovery-lead policy

Homepages, listings, search pages, login pages, jobs, and spam are hard non-content. Social posts and secondary hosting pages are not automatically candidates; when they contain a credible pointer to substantial original material, they become `discovery_lead` plus `original_source_required`.

A discovery lead cannot consume a daily candidate slot. It can only produce a candidate after an independent original article or document is recovered.

## 8. Source-resolution policy

Resolution order:

1. explicit canonical/original URL in metadata or body;
2. named original publisher, wire service, report, speech, or document;
3. credible hosting source when no separate original source exists;
4. otherwise `uncertain`.

Wire attribution and translation relationships must survive canonical replacement. For example:

```text
hosting_source = VernonReporter
canonical_source = Associated Press
wire_service = AP
source_relationship = wire_republish
```

## 9. Duplicate policy

Clustering proceeds from strongest to weakest evidence:

1. identical canonical URL;
2. identical content hash;
3. same wire service plus normalized title/body evidence;
4. explicit translated-edition relationship;
5. near-duplicate normalized title and body fingerprint;
6. same-event low-increment editorial grouping.

Items 21, 46, and 48 in the ground-truth batch must resolve to one AP wire cluster.

## 10. Release gates

### Transport gate

Scheduling, search, extraction, cache writing, and basic volume are operational.

### Editorial gate

Before v0.4 can be considered ready:

- overall 48-item disposition accuracy >= 85%;
- candidate precision >= 85%;
- source-chase recall >= 6/7;
- critical false accepts = 0 for jobs, login pages, homepages, channel pages, and spam;
- AP wire-cluster accuracy for items 21/46/48 = 100%;
- item 43 remains a labeled translated candidate;
- item 47 remains a primary-document special candidate.

### Promotion gate

`READY` only when transport and editorial gates are both `READY`, followed by at least three consecutive days of v0.4 shadow A/B validation. Promotion is manual; automatic promotion remains disabled.

## 11. Sheet and runtime ownership

- `collector_ground_truth`: release evaluation and regression only.
- `article_cache`: current article and lead records.
- `collector_runs`: runtime and disposition counts.
- `collector_health`: transport, editorial, and promotion gates.
- `source_registry`: directed-source execution state.
- `manual_review`: future ad-hoc item feedback only; superseded batch rows are ignored.
