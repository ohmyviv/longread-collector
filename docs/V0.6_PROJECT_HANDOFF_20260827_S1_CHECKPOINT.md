## 3. S1 evidence contract

Day-0 acceptance and Route utility evaluation are deliberately separated.

```text
L0 Scheduler / cohort availability
L1 Control validity
L2 Experimental isolation
L3 Telemetry integrity
-------------------------------
L4 Route technical health
L5 Route utility evidence
```

L0–L3 determine whether a prospective sample is valid evidence. L4–L5 describe technical health and incremental supply but do not by themselves authorize route promotion.

The denominator is **eligible exposure per naturally selected source**, not a raw count of 3–5 runs. A source exposure is eligible only when the natural Control run is in the prospective cohort and the Control/isolation/telemetry evidence is valid. Pre-activation historical runs are excluded from the failure denominator.
