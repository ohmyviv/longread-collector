# v0.6 PR-7.1 Quality Calibration

## Why this PR exists

The first valid full-parallel natural shadow sample after PR-7 was the scheduled
`zh_midday` run on 2026-08-08 (`COL-20260808-124058-BJT-zh_midday`).  Engineering
integrity passed: the control and shadow shared the full discovery snapshot and
acquired bodies, with no shadow discovery/extraction requests, no shadow
Firecrawl requests, and no incremental acquisition cost.

Quality did not pass.  The run therefore counts as a valid diagnostic Natural
Shadow Evidence Day, but not as a Release Acceptance Day.

The main observed failure modes mapped cleanly to existing v0.6 layers:

1. Canonical publication facts could still prefer a stale site/template timestamp
   over a local article publication date.
2. Editorial semantic risk was sometimes inflated by navigation/footer/template
   text and by generic Chinese words occurring inside legitimate reporting.
3. Portfolio expected utility treated freshness as a soft penalty, allowing known
   stale ordinary longreads to survive if their other scores were high enough.

PR-7.1 addresses only those boundaries.  It does not alter the six-layer
architecture or broaden collector scope.

## Canonical publication calibration

Publication resolution remains a factual canonical responsibility.

Evidence is ranked by provenance:

- explicit article/body publication evidence;
- labeled publication evidence near the article header;
- other resolved page metadata;
- acquisition raw dates;
- discovery date hints.

Article-local publication evidence outranks generic page/template timestamps.
All credible candidates remain auditable.  If multiple high-confidence
article-local publication dates disagree, the conflict is explicit and the
resolved publication confidence is capped.  The canonical freshness facts also
store resolved age in days for downstream policy use.

A date conflict is not itself an editorial rejection.  It is a factual
uncertainty that downstream policy can defer.

## Editorial risk calibration

The PR-3 Editorial Judge remains the baseline implementation.  PR-7.1 applies a
bounded calibration only when the baseline shows material risk inflation.

The calibration finds the article-local content region using the resolved title,
trims common related-content/footer regions, and evaluates strong promotional,
event, and transcript cues inside that region.

Generic words such as `打造`, `赋能`, `助力`, `推动`, or a single mention of a
conference are not sufficient for high promotional/event risk.

Canonical intrinsic facts remain authoritative:

- event listing / event preview / event recap remain high event risk;
- promotion genre remains high promotional risk;
- television/podcast transcript remains high transcript risk;
- market/data-card material remains high template/data-product risk.

Thus this is not a relaxation of structural safety gates.  It is a reduction of
whole-page semantic false positives.

## Standard-longread freshness boundary

Freshness becomes a policy safety boundary for the ordinary longread track:

- age <= 7 days: normal expected-utility portfolio selection;
- age 8-14 days: only a deep-read exception;
- age > 14 days: reject from the standard-longread track;
- missing or materially conflicting publication date: defer rather than use a
  neutral freshness score.

The 8-14 day deep-read exception requires:

- Editorial verdict `recommend`;
- substance >= 0.88;
- composite depth/value score >= 0.85;
- max semantic/template risk < 0.45;
- marginal utility greater than the weakest selected timely standard longread;
- maximum two deep-read exceptions per run.

Primary documents, academic papers, and explicit source-chase actions remain on
separate tracks and are not forced through the ordinary-longread freshness rule.

## Regression policy

PR-7.1 adds structural regression cases rather than URL/title exceptions:

- article-local recent date versus stale template timestamp;
- conflicting local publication dates;
- English `Created` publication evidence;
- navigation/footer promotion/event noise around a substantive reported article;
- intrinsic event recap remains rejected;
- very stale ordinary longread cannot be rescued by high utility;
- unknown/conflicting standard date defers;
- old academic material stays on the academic track;
- 8-14 day deep-read quality floor and two-item cap.

## Invariants / non-goals

PR-7.1 does not:

- add sources;
- raise the 32-attempt acquisition cap;
- raise Firecrawl budget;
- change v0.5.6m legacy-control behavior;
- connect v0.6 output to the 07:35 editor;
- enable automatic promotion.

## Shadow acceptance reset

A semantic change resets consecutive release-acceptance counting.

The 2026-08-08 `zh_midday` sample remains retained as diagnostic evidence.  After
PR-7.1 is merged and frozen, the next qualifying scheduled natural run starts a
new Release Acceptance Day 1 candidate.  Promotion remains manual and still
requires all previously defined release gates, including candidate precision,
zero serious false accepts, recall, audit closure, and consecutive natural
shadow days.
