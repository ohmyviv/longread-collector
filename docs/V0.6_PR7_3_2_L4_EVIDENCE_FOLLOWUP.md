## Blocker A — explicit original-source links were missed

Several natural pages were hosted by an aggregator or republisher but contained direct, explicit source evidence.

Observed examples included:

- Huxiu: `本文来自微信公众号：[硅星人Pro](...)`
- Huxiu: `本文来自微信公众号：[宇众不同的露萱](...)`
- ByDrug: title-adjacent `[查看原文](https://www.vbdata.cn/...)`

PR-7.3 source resolution recognized a narrower set of labels such as `来源`, `稿源`, `原载`, and `转载自`. The above natural wording therefore remained `source_relationship=original` even when an external original URL was explicit.

### PR-7.3.2 rule

Add only high-precision explicit cues:

1. a publisher-named WeChat attribution of the form `本文/文章/稿件...来自/来源于/转载自 微信公众号：[publisher](url)` in the early article region;
2. a generic `[查看原文]`, `[原文链接]`, or `[原文]` link only inside a tight title-local window.

The target must be a credible external URL. Ordinary external links and late references are not source evidence.

When triggered:

- `source_relationship = secondary_republish`
- `source_action = replace_with_original`
- `canonical_content_url = explicit original URL`
- named publisher attribution, when present, becomes the canonical/original publisher label.

Existing wire and translated-republish semantics have higher precedence and are preserved.

## Blocker B — title-adjacent standalone absolute datetime was not promoted

The ByDrug natural page displayed:

`2026-08-10 08:00 [查看原文](...)`

immediately below the article title. The date was real and article-local, but it did not use one of the labeled forms recognized by PR-7.3 (`发布时间`, `发布日期`, `Published`, etc.). The remaining evidence was weak enough that L6 correctly kept `publication_date_unknown_defer`.

The L6 policy was not the defect; L4 failed to preserve the strength of an explicit title-local publication fact.

### PR-7.3.2 rule

Recognize one additional positive date form:

- a standalone numeric absolute date or datetime on its own line within a **tight title-local window**.

Examples:

- `2026-08-10`
- `2026-08-10 08:00`
- `2026/08/10 08:00`
- `2026年08月10日 08:00`

The evidence is emitted as `body_header_standalone_datetime` with article-local provenance.

This rule deliberately does **not** restore the old broad standalone-date scan. The PR-7.3.1 C&EN protection remains: a date in a Related card thousands of characters later cannot become article-local merely because it appears somewhere in the acquired body.
